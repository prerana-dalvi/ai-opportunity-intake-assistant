# AI Opportunity Intake Brief: Provider Call Center Policy Assistant

**Business area:** Provider Services  
**Workflow domain:** Provider Services / Contact Center  
**AI patterns:** Knowledge Retrieval (RAG), User Assistance (Copilot/Chat), Workflow Automation  
**Sponsor:** Director, Provider Services

## Use case summary
Provider Services teams face this challenge: Call center agents spend minutes searching provider manuals and billing rules to answer provider inquiries about claim denials and timely filing, causing long hold times and call abandonment. This opportunity applies Knowledge Retrieval (RAG), User Assistance (Copilot/Chat), Workflow Automation within Provider Services / Contact Center to assist agents with cited answers in seconds to improve first-contact resolution and reduce abandon rate.

## Business value hypothesis
We believe an AI assistant that can search approved policy manuals and billing rules and return a cited answer in seconds will save about 900 staff hours per month for Provider Services, measured by answer accuracy on golden question set; average handle time and cycle time.

Estimated capacity released: **~900 hrs/month (~5.62 FTE)** — planning estimate, validate against baseline.

## Initial solution concept
An AI assistant will search approved policy manuals and billing rules and return a cited answer in seconds. Staff remain in control: the assistant suggests, a person confirms. Every suggestion is logged with its source so supervisors can audit it. A pilot with one team validates accuracy and adoption before scaling.

## Draft requirements
- Secure, read-only integration with Provider billing manual, Policy bulletins, FAQ knowledge base
- Human review and override for every AI suggestion, with reason codes
- Audit log of inputs, outputs, model version and reviewer action
- Role-based access aligned to HIPAA minimum-necessary
- Baseline metrics captured before pilot (volume, handle time, error rate)
- Evaluation dataset and acceptance thresholds agreed with business owner

## Success metrics
- Answer accuracy on golden question set; average handle time
- First-contact resolution; escalation rate; user satisfaction (CSAT)
- Touchless / straight-through processing rate
- Staff hours saved per month (target ~900)
