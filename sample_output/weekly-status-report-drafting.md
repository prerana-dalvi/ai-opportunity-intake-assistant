## Use case summary
PMO teams face this challenge: Project leads manually copy status updates from Jira into a weekly client report. This opportunity applies Workflow Automation, Decision Support, Knowledge Retrieval (RAG) within General Operations to draft the report automatically for lead review.

## Business value hypothesis
We believe an AI assistant that can route, pre-fill and update work items automatically, removing manual re-keying will save about 30 staff hours per month for PMO, measured by touchless / straight-through processing rate and cycle time.

Estimated capacity released: **~30 hrs/month (~0.19 FTE)** — planning estimate, validate against baseline.

## Initial solution concept
An AI assistant will route, pre-fill and update work items automatically, removing manual re-keying. Staff remain in control: the assistant suggests, a person confirms. Every suggestion is logged with its source so supervisors can audit it. A pilot with one team validates accuracy and adoption before scaling.

## Draft requirements
- Secure, read-only integration with Jira
- Human review and override for every AI suggestion, with reason codes
- Audit log of inputs, outputs, model version and reviewer action
- Role-based access aligned to HIPAA minimum-necessary
- Baseline metrics captured before pilot (volume, handle time, error rate)
- Evaluation dataset and acceptance thresholds agreed with business owner

## Success metrics
- Touchless / straight-through processing rate
- Reviewer agreement rate with AI ranking; time-to-decision
- Answer accuracy on golden question set; average handle time
- Staff hours saved per month (target ~30)
