## Use case summary
Utilization Management teams face this challenge: Nurse reviewers read long faxed medical records to determine medical necessity for prior auth requests. This opportunity applies Document Intelligence, Decision Support within Prior Authorization / UM to summarize relevant clinical notes against criteria to assist reviewers and meet turnaround SLAs.

## Business value hypothesis
We believe an AI assistant that can extract key fields from incoming documents, validate them against system data, and pre-populate the work item will save about 625 staff hours per month for Utilization Management, measured by field-extraction accuracy (target >= 95% on validation set) and cycle time.

Estimated capacity released: **~625 hrs/month (~3.91 FTE)** — planning estimate, validate against baseline.

## Initial solution concept
An AI assistant will extract key fields from incoming documents, validate them against system data, and pre-populate the work item. Staff remain in control: the assistant suggests, a person confirms. Every suggestion is logged with its source so supervisors can audit it. A pilot with one team validates accuracy and adoption before scaling.
