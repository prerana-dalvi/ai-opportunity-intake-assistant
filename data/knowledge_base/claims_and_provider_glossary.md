# Claims and Provider Operations Glossary (sample)

General industry definitions written for this project; check the governing state contract and provider manual for program-specific rules.

## Timely filing
The deadline by which a provider must submit a claim after the date of service. Claims received after the timely filing limit are typically denied unless an approved exception applies. Limits vary by state Medicaid program.

## Pended claim
A claim that adjudication could not finalize automatically because an edit or audit fired. Pended claims wait in a work queue for an examiner to resolve, deny or release them.

## Claim edits and audits
Automated rules applied during adjudication. Edits check a single claim (for example a missing NPI or an invalid procedure code). Audits compare a claim with claim history (for example duplicate billing or frequency limits).

## Provider enrollment and revalidation
Enrollment is the process by which a provider applies to participate in a Medicaid program. Federal rules require Medicaid providers to revalidate their enrollment at least every five years, which creates periodic volume spikes.

## NPI
The National Provider Identifier is a 10-digit identifier assigned to health care providers under HIPAA administrative simplification.

## Prior authorization
A requirement that a provider obtain approval before delivering certain services. Reviewers check the request against clinical criteria to determine medical necessity.

## MMIS
The Medicaid Management Information System is the claims processing and information retrieval system each state uses to administer its Medicaid program.

## Remittance advice (835)
The electronic remittance advice transaction that tells a provider how each claim was paid, adjusted or denied. Claims are submitted electronically using the 837 transaction.
